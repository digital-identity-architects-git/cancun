Golden Days on the Caribbean

Spend your mornings snorkeling the Mesoamerican Reef, swimming in jungle cenotes, or relaxing on the white sand of the Hotel Zone. With 300+ sunny days a year and water that hovers around 28°C, every day in Cancún is made for the beach.

Cancún sits on the Caribbean coast of the Yucatán Peninsula in Quintana Roo, Mexico — a launchpad for Chichén Itzá, Tulum, Isla Mujeres and Cozumel.

See Daytime Activities

Nights That Never Fade

As the sky deepens, Cancún's nightlife switches on. Dine on fresh ceviche and cochinita pibil, sail a sunset catamaran, or dance until sunrise at the Hotel Zone's legendary clubs like Coco Bongo.

However you like to spend your evenings, the city has a rhythm waiting for you.
