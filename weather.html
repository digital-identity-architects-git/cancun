When to Go

🌟

Best Overall: Dec–April

The dry season brings sunshine, low humidity and minimal seaweed — but also peak prices and crowds.

💸

Best Value: May, Nov

Shoulder months balance good weather with smaller crowds and lower resort rates.

🐋

Whale Sharks: Jun–Sep

Summer is hot and humid but the only time to snorkel with whale sharks offshore.

Hurricane & Sargassum Seasons

Hurricane season runs June through November, peaking in September–October. Direct hits are rare, but pack flexibility and consider travel insurance if visiting late summer.

Sargassum (seaweed) season typically peaks from May to August, when brown seaweed can wash ashore. The north-facing beaches of the Hotel Zone and nearby Isla Mujeres usually stay clearer, and resorts rake the sand daily.

Water temperature stays a swimmable 26–29°C (79–84°F) all year.
