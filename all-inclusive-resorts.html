What's Usually Included?

Cancún all-inclusive packages typically cover your room, all meals and snacks, unlimited drinks (including alcohol), non-motorized watersports, pools, entertainment and taxes & gratuities. Higher tiers add à-la-carte dining, premium liquor, butler service and spa credits.

Many packages bundle airfare and airport transfers, and some include resort credits for tours, the spa or romantic dinners.
