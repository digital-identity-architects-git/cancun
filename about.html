About Cancún

The story behind the Mexican Caribbean's most famous beach city.

Paradise, by Design

Cancún sits on the northeastern tip of Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, in the state of Quintana Roo, where the turquoise Caribbean Sea meets miles of white-sand beach. Just decades ago it was a quiet barrier island; today it's one of the world's most visited beach destinations.

Developed as a planned resort city in the 1970s, Cancún paired its postcard coastline with easy access to ancient Maya wonders, cenotes and the wider Riviera Maya — a combination few destinations can match.

What Makes Cancún Special

🏖️

The Beaches

Soft white coral sand and impossibly clear water stretch 22 km along the Hotel Zone.

🏛️

Maya Heritage

Chichén Itzá, Tulum and El Rey put two thousand years of history within easy reach.

🌎

Easy Access

One of Latin America's busiest airports makes Cancún a simple, direct trip from North America and Europe.
