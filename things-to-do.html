Things to Do in Cancún

Adventure by day, magic by night — here's how to make the most of every moment.

By Day ☀️

Reefs, ruins and lagoons — the Caribbean sun brings endless ways to play.

🤿

Snorkel the Reef

Explore the Mesoamerican Reef — the second largest in the world — teeming with rays, turtles and tropical fish.

🏛️

Visit Chichén Itzá

Stand before El Castillo, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World, just a couple of hours inland.

💧

Swim in a Cenote

Dive into crystal-clear sinkholes carved into the jungle — sacred and stunning natural pools.

🛥️

Isla Mujeres Day Trip

Hop a ferry to this laid-back island for golf-cart cruising, beach bars and calm swimming bays.

🐢

Xel-Há & Eco Parks

Float lazy rivers, zip-line through the jungle and snorkel in natural lagoons at the region's eco-parks.

🏖️

Beach Hop the Zona

Playa Delfines, Playa Norte and more — find your perfect patch of white sand along the Hotel Zone.

Make a Day of It

Most tours and excursions can be booked the day before, but popular trips to Chichén Itzá and Tulum sell out fast in high season. Start early to beat both the crowds and the midday heat.

Ask About Tours

By Night 🌙

When the sun dips below the horizon, Cancún truly comes alive.

🍸

Rooftop & Beach Clubs

Sip cocktails with ocean views or dance under the stars at the Hotel Zone's legendary nightspots.

🌮

Taste the Yucatán

Feast on cochinita pibil, fresh ceviche and street tacos at downtown markets and seaside restaurants.

🎭

Live Shows & Culture

Catch dazzling dinner shows celebrating Mexican music, dance and history under the night sky.

🚤

Sunset Sailing

Toast the golden hour aboard a catamaran cruise across the Nichupté Lagoon and open sea.

✨

Stargazing on the Beach

Escape the lights for a quieter night — clear Caribbean skies make for unforgettable stargazing.

🎶

Live Music Bars

From salsa to reggae, downtown's intimate venues serve up rhythm and local flavor every night.

The City After Dark

Cancún's nightlife ranges from glamorous mega-clubs to mellow beachfront lounges. Whatever your pace, the evening glow over the water is unforgettable.
