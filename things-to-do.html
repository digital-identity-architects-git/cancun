Things to Do in Cancún

Attractions, cenotes, Maya ruins and nightlife — how to make the most of every moment.

Top Attractions & Excursions ☀️

Reefs, ruins and lagoons — the Caribbean sun brings endless things to do in and around Cancún.

🤿

Snorkeling the Reef

Explore the Mesoamerican Reef — the second largest in the world — on a Cancún snorkeling tour full of rays, turtles and tropical fish.

🏛️

Chichén Itzá Tour

Stand before El Castillo, one of the New Seven Wonders, on a day trip from Cancún to Chichén Itzá and the Ik Kil cenote.

💧

Cenotes

Swim in crystal-clear cenotes carved into the jungle — sacred natural sinkholes on a Cancún cenote excursion.

🏝️

Isla Mujeres

Take the ferry to Isla Mujeres for golf-cart cruising, Playa Norte and calm swimming bays — an easy Cancún day trip.

🐋

Swim with Whale Sharks

From June to September, join a whale shark tour to snorkel beside the ocean's gentle giants off the coast.

🎢

Xcaret & Eco Parks

Float underground rivers, zip-line and snorkel at Xcaret, Xel-Há, Xplor and Xenses eco-parks near Cancún.

🐬

Swim with Dolphins

Meet dolphins at Isla Mujeres or the Hotel Zone — a favorite Cancún activity for families.

🖼️

Underwater Museum (MUSA)

Dive or snorkel the MUSA underwater museum, where hundreds of sculptures form an artificial reef.

🏖️

Beach Hopping

Playa Delfines, Playa Norte and more — find your perfect patch of white sand along the Zona Hotelera.

Make a Day of It

Most Cancún tours and excursions can be booked a day ahead, but popular trips to Chichén Itzá, Tulum and the whale sharks sell out fast in high season. Start early to beat both the crowds and the midday heat.

Cancún Nightlife & Dining 🌙

When the sun dips below the horizon, Cancún truly comes alive.

🪩

Coco Bongo & Clubs

Dance through the night at Coco Bongo and the Hotel Zone's legendary clubs and beach parties.

🌮

Taste the Yucatán

Feast on cochinita pibil, fresh ceviche and street tacos at downtown markets and the best Cancún restaurants.

⛵

Sunset Catamaran Cruise

Toast the golden hour on a catamaran or booze cruise across the Nichupté Lagoon and open sea.

🚤

Pirate Ship Show

Family-friendly dinner cruises and pirate ship adventures sail nightly from the marina.

🎭

Live Shows & Culture

Catch dazzling dinner shows celebrating Mexican music, dance and history under the night sky.

🍸

Rooftop & Beach Bars

Sip cocktails with ocean views or find a quieter beach lounge for a mellow Cancún evening.

The City After Dark

Cancún's nightlife ranges from glamorous mega-clubs to mellow beachfront lounges. Whatever your pace, the evening glow over the water is unforgettable.
