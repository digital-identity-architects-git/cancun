Getting to Cancún

Flights, the airport, transfers and how to get around once you land.

Flights to Cancún (CUN)

Cancún International Airport (CUN) is one of the busiest in Latin America, with direct flights from across the US, Canada, Mexico and Europe.

✈️

Direct Flights

Nonstop service from hubs like Dallas, Houston, NYC, LAX, Miami, Toronto and Mexico City keeps Cancún easy to reach.

💲

Cheap Flights

Fares dip in the September–October low season. Compare round-trip deals and set fare alerts for the best prices.

🛬

The Airport

CUN has four terminals; most international flights use T3 and T4. Allow extra time for immigration in high season.

Airport Transfers & Transportation

From the airport to your hotel, you have several options — book transfers ahead to skip the timeshare touts at arrivals.

🚐

Private & Shared Transfers

Pre-booked private or shared shuttles are the easiest way to reach the Hotel Zone (about 20–30 min) or Playa del Carmen and Tulum further south.

🚌

ADO Bus

The ADO bus is a cheap, reliable option from the airport to downtown Cancún, Playa del Carmen and Tulum.

🚗

Car Rental

Renting a car suits road trips to cenotes, ruins and the Riviera Maya — just budget for mandatory Mexican insurance.

🚕

Taxi & Uber

Authorized taxis and rideshare are available; agree on fares or use the app, and keep small bills for tips.

Getting to Nearby Destinations

Cancún is the gateway to the Riviera Maya. From the airport or Hotel Zone you can reach Playa del Carmen (~1 hr), Tulum (~1.5–2 hrs) and Isla Mujeres by ferry from Puerto Juárez or the Ultramar terminal (~20 min crossing).

Day trips to Chichén Itzá (~2.5 hrs) and Cozumel (ferry from Playa del Carmen) are popular and easy to arrange.
