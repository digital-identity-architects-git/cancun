Cancún Travel Tips

Safety, money, the time zone and everything to know before you go.

Know Before You Go

Quick answers to the questions Cancún travelers ask most.

🛡️

Is Cancún Safe?

The Hotel Zone and main tourist areas are heavily policed and generally safe. Use the same common sense you would in any big city: stick to official taxis, watch your drinks, and check your government's current travel advisory before you go.

🕐

Time Zone

Cancún uses Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC−5) year-round and does not observe daylight saving, so it can differ from US Eastern by an hour in summer.

💵

Currency & Money

The Mexican peso (MXN) is the local currency. US dollars are widely accepted in the Hotel Zone, but you'll get better value paying in pesos. Cards are common; carry small bills for tips.

🧾

VisiTax

Quintana Roo charges a small tourist tax (VisiTax) per visitor. Pay it online before you fly home to save time at the airport.

🚰

Drinking Water

Stick to bottled or filtered water. Resorts provide purified water, and reusable bottles with filters are handy for day trips.

🔌

Power & Plugs

Mexico uses 127V and the same Type A/B plugs as the US and Canada, so most North American devices need no adapter.

What to Pack

  • 🧴 Reef-safe sunscreen (required at cenotes and eco-parks)
  • 🩴 Swimwear, sandals and a cover-up
  • 🕶️ Sunglasses, hat and a light rain layer in summer
  • 🦟 Insect repellent for jungle and ruins trips
  • 👟 Water shoes for rocky cenotes and reefs
  • 💳 A mix of pesos, cards and small bills for tips

A Quick Map & Orientation

Cancún sits on the northeast tip of the Yucatán Peninsula in Quintana Roo, Mexico, on the Caribbean Sea.

🏖️

Hotel Zone

The "7"-shaped barrier island (Zona Hotelera) holds the beaches, resorts and nightlife along Boulevard Kukulcán.

🌆

Downtown

El Centro, inland to the west, is the working city with markets, the ADO bus station and local eats.

🧭

The Region

South lies the Riviera Maya (Playa del Carmen, Tulum); offshore are Isla Mujeres and Cozumel.
